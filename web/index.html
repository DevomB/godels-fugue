Canon Collapse

Composes canons as a constraint problem and proves why every note is there. Pick a piece and press Compose. Click any note to see the rules that put it there, or give it another pitch and hear the piece re-compose around your choice. This is the C program compiled to WebAssembly, running in your browser.
