# The demo's opening piece: a three-voice canon in D minor on the eighth-note
# grid. Each voice enters three bars after the one before and an octave lower,
# so the melody is heard high, middle and low (a soprano, a tenor and a bass
# register) over a chord per bar that lines up as i-iv-V-i. The lines move
# mostly by step (w_step), eighth notes run by step (w_run), and leading
# tones rise to the tonic.
grid eighth
length 64
voices 3
delay 24
transpose_1 -12
transpose_2 -24
key D
mode minor
harmony 1
rhythm 1
max_hold 3
max_rests 1
leading_tone 1
range_low 43
range_high 88
w_step 3
w_gravity 0
w_repeat 6
w_contrary 1
w_hold 0
tempo 84
